# Character Tags

The trademark catalog for character creation. Choose one Past, one Present, and one Perk trademark (see the Rules, section 1). The listed edges and flaws are only suggestions.

## Past Trademarks

What the character did before joining the crew. A Past trademark applies whenever that training, those habits, or those contacts are relevant.

**Advocate**
You practiced law: company counsel, colonial courts, public defense.
*Edges*: contracts, court procedure, cross-examination, making a deal, finding the loophole.
*Flaws*: disbarred, in a syndicate's pocket, everyone hates lawyers.

**Chaplain**
You tended souls: void missions, station parishes, hospital wards.
*Edges*: last rites, counsel, a congregation in every port, scripture, calming a crowd, hearing confessions.
*Flaws*: doubt, defrocked, zealous

**Company Spook**
You did intelligence work for a corporation or a government.
*Edges*: surveillance, forged credentials, dead drops, tailing, turning assets, disappearing.
*Flaws*: loose ends being tied, trusts no one, a cover identity that won't die, knows too much.

**Contract Killer**
You killed for money, or for a cause.
*Edges*: quiet entries, untraceable weapons, cold reads, patience, disposal, looking harmless.
*Flaws*: the last job went wrong, a name in the wrong ledgers, wanted, getting sentimental.

**Convict**
You did time: company pens, labor colonies, prison hulks.
*Edges*: prison economy, improvised weapons, keeping your head down, hard bargains, reading a room, enduring the worst.
*Flaws*: parole conditions, an old cellmate resurfaces, institutionalized, known to security everywhere.

**Corporate**
You worked the office side: management, finance, compliance.
*Edges*: contracts, bureaucracy, reading an org chart, boardroom manners, expense accounts, finding the loophole.
*Flaws*: non-compete clause, soft hands,  still thinks like the company.

**Cult Survivor**
You got out of one of the void churches. Mostly.
*Edges*: doctrine, recognizing the signs, hiding in plain sight, reading true believers, ritual details.
*Flaws*: they want you back, litanies you can't unhear, can't enter a chapel, still half believing.

**Dockhand**
You worked the docks and freight decks.
*Edges*: cargo handling, loaders and cranes, rigging and lashing, union contacts, brawling, knowing what's in the crates.
*Flaws*: drinks on the job, a ruined back, owes the union, quick to anger.

**Drifter**
You moved from station to station, working whatever came.
*Edges*: scrounging, odd jobs, sleeping anywhere, hearing things, well-travelled, going unnoticed.
*Flaws*: no fixed address, on the run, can't stay put, night terrors.

**Engineer**
You kept drives, reactors, and life support running.
*Edges*: jury-rigging, damage control, drive systems, reactor protocols, diagnosis by ear, cannibalizing parts.
*Flaws*: never off the clock, trusts machines over people, signed off on something bad, union blacklist.

**Grifter**
You lived by the con: swindles, cards, false fronts.
*Edges*: smooth talk, reading a mark, forged papers, sleight of hand, playing a role, the long game.
*Flaws*: a mark remembers, greedy, smart mouth, can't play it straight.

**Hacker**
You made a living inside other people's systems.
*Edges*: intrusion, signal spoofing, scrubbing records, hardware taps, social engineering, old backdoors.
*Flaws*: a handle someone remembers, warrants outstanding, paranoid about surveillance, can't resist a locked system.

**Marine**
You served: boarding parties, drop operations, station garrisons.
*Edges*: boarding actions, fireteam tactics, zero-g combat, breach charges, weapons maintenance, calm under fire.
*Flaws*: follows orders, war nightmares, discharged with prejudice, an old unit's grudge.

**Marshal**
You enforced the law: station security, the marshal service, company police.
*Edges*: interrogation, procedure, spotting trouble, holds and cuffs, working informants, how cops think.
*Flaws*: enemies on the force, can't let a case go, reads as a cop, bent once.

**Miner**
You cut rock and ice: asteroid rigs, moon shafts, deep bores.
*Edges*: demolitions, heavy machinery, vacuum work, reading rock, ore assay, fast suit patches.
*Flaws*: bad lungs, scrip debt, survivor of a cave-in, deaf in one ear.

**Organizer**
You built the union, one meeting at a time.
*Edges*: rabble-rousing, strike logistics, clandestine meetings, sabotage, a crowd's mood, paperwork as a weapon.
*Flaws*: blacklisted, the cause comes first, marked by company security, a burned local.

**Performer**
You worked crowds: circuit entertainer, feed personality, lounge act.
*Edges*: captivating a crowd, disguise, reading an audience, voices and accents, improvisation, name recognition.
*Flaws*: recognized at the wrong time, craves an audience, an old feud, booking-agency debts.

**Physician**
You practiced medicine: company clinics, colony hospitals, ship sickbays.
*Edges*: surgery, pharmacology, triage, cryo revival, bedside manner, a glance diagnosis.
*Flaws*: license troubles, samples the pharmacy, the oath gets in the way, med-school debt.

**Pilot**
You flew for a living: freight, shuttles, tugs, racing, a military wing.
*Edges*: hard burns, docking, evasive maneuvers, orbital mechanics, dead reckoning, unfamiliar controls.
*Flaws*: license revoked, stim habit, showboat, lost a wingman.

**Scientist**
You did research: corporate labs, survey missions, universities.
*Edges*: analysis, instrumentation, a field of study (choose one), peer contacts, designing experiments, technical writing.
*Flaws*: scooped and bitter, an ethics finding, obsessed with the problem, funding strings.

**Smuggler**
You moved cargo past people paid to stop it.
*Edges*: hidden compartments, customs tricks, cool under inspection, forged manifests, the quiet routes, gray-market contacts.
*Flaws*: burned a partner, owes a syndicate, on a watchlist, can't refuse an easy run.

**Steward**
You served aboard liners and corporate habitats: cook, porter, cabin crew.
*Edges*: etiquette, gossip, going unnoticed, keys to everywhere, reading guests, endurance.
*Flaws*: sticky fingers, bad references, invisible to the powerful, loose talk.

**Street Rat**
You grew up in the gutters of stations and colony blocks.
*Edges*: picking pockets, gutter contacts, sniffing out marks, vanishing into crowds, duct shortcuts, surviving on nothing.
*Flaws*: no schooling, hoards food, the old gang remembers, steals out of habit.

**Stringer**
You chased stories for the feeds.
*Edges*: working sources, getting the footage, asking around, spotting a story, publishing leverage, tailing.
*Flaws*: byline enemies, never off the record, chases the story too far, the archive owns your work.

**Vat Tender**
You grew things: hydroponics, protein vats, agridomes.
*Edges*: hydroponics, biotech vats, pest control, feedstock chemistry, patience, making anything grow.
*Flaws*: smells of the vats, samples the product, soft on living things, a recall with your name on it.

**Wrecker**
You broke ships and stripped derelicts for salvage.
*Edges*: cutting torches, spotting value, salvage law, a feel for derelicts, working in wrecks, rigging tows.
*Flaws*: superstitious about dead ships, claim-jumped a rival, grim souvenirs, heard something in a hulk once.

## Present Trademarks

The role the character plays on a job. Present trademarks are functions, not occupations: a former marine might serve as Defender, Enforcer, or Scout.

**Charmer**
You get what the crew needs with charm, looks, and lies.
*Edges*: fast talk, disarming warmth, reading a room, distraction, seduction, straight-faced lies, disguise.
*Flaws*: overconfident, recognizable, a sleazy reputation, falls for the mark.

**Defender**
You keep the crew alive when things go wrong.
*Edges*: covering fire, bodyguarding, holding chokepoints, spotting threats, dragging people clear, disarming.
*Flaws*: nobody left behind, slow to stand down, sees threats everywhere, keeps score of every scar.

**Enforcer**
You apply violence and the threat of it.
*Edges*: intimidation, gunfights, dirty fighting, making examples, sizing someone up, nerves of steel.
*Flaws*: force first, a reputation for going too far, soft-hearted underneath, wanted.

**Finder**
You get things: goods, people, information.
*Edges*: black markets, appraisal, knowing who has it, calling in favors, haggling, tracking someone down.
*Flaws*: everything's for sale, favors owed everywhere, single-minded, can't resist a bargain.

**Infiltrator**
You get in and out without being seen.
*Edges*: locks and seals, silent movement, security systems, going unnoticed, climbing and crawling, escapes.
*Flaws*: works alone, takes souvenirs, curiosity opens the wrong doors, a record.

**Investigator**
You work out what happened and who did it.
*Edges*: working a scene, following money, tracing ships, connecting dots, interrogation, a memory for faces.
*Flaws*: can't let it go, sees patterns that aren't there, asks noticeable questions, a personal code.

**Leader**
You make the plan and hold the crew together.
*Edges*: command, making a plan, calming a situation, inspiring others, delegation, reading the crew.
*Flaws*: cares too much, people as pieces, overthinks, takes every loss personally.

**Negotiator**
You talk: deals, truces, prices, terms.
*Edges*: making a deal, de-escalation, bribes, contracts, sensing motives, knowing when to walk.
*Flaws*: keeps talking too long, splits every difference, greedy, a sucker for a sob story.

**Overwatch**
You run comms and sensors while the crew works.
*Edges*: running comms, sensor watch, guiding movement, jamming and spoofing, drone eyes, calling the abort.
*Flaws*: never in the room, second-guesses the plan, carries every loss on the channel, helpless at a distance.

**Quartermaster**
You keep the crew supplied, stowed, and fed.
*Edges*: logistics, rationing, stowage, stretching supplies, cooking for a crew, knowing where everything is.
*Flaws*: counts every round, hates waste, ledger grudges, feeds strays.

**Researcher**
You turn archives, samples, and manuals into answers.
*Edges*: archives, lab work, technical manuals, patient analysis, cross-referencing, forensics.
*Flaws*: needs more data, lectures, hoards findings, precise to a fault.

**Scout**
You go first and report back.
*Edges*: EVA surveys, sensor sweeps, taking point, mapping as you go, moving light, noticing what changed.
*Flaws*: ranges too far ahead, restless in port, first through every door, trusts eyes over instruments.

## Perk Trademarks

An advantage: a special ability, quality, possession, or place. Cybernetic modifications are edges under Chromed or Wired.

**Analytical**
You process details like a machine.
*Edges*: cold logic, pattern spotting, running the numbers, total recall, spotting the flaw, planning ahead.
*Flaws*: blunt, overthinks under fire, needs the full picture, people are not numbers.

**Android**
You are a synthetic person, owned or free.
*Edges*: doesn't breathe, inhuman calm, direct data interface, stronger than you look, perfect recall, running without rest.
*Flaws*: corporate property, behavioral governors, uncanny to strangers, parts are hard to find.

**Big**
You are very large.
*Edges*: strength, reach, taking a hit, fists like mag-clamps, carrying the load, an intimidating silhouette.
*Flaws*: the first target, nothing fits, clumsy in tight spaces, a big softy.

**Chromed**
You carry body modifications. Take specific mods as edges.
*Edges*: targeting optics, subdermal plating, tool fingers, reinforced frame, mag-grip hands, an internal compartment.
*Flaws*: scanner flags, maintenance debts, phantom pains, shock-sensitive systems.

**Connected**
You know people everywhere.
*Edges*: a cousin on every station, favors owed, hearing things early, introductions, vouching, finding the local fixer.
*Flaws*: favors cut both ways, gossip travels back, family asks too much, guilt by association.

**Credentials**
You hold an official-looking authorization: an inspector's badge, a steward's pin, a corporate lanyard.
*Edges*: opening doors, a little authority, getting waved through, requisitions.
*Flaws*: the real owners notice, animosity of real inspectors, an expiry date, impersonation charges.

**Deadeye**
You are a capable shot.
*Edges*: quick draw, precise aim, long shots, steady hands, weapon maintenance, trick shots.
*Flaws*: a show-off, a personal code, counts every kill, hands shake afterward.

**Dirty Fighter**
When it comes to blows, you hit low.
*Edges*: low blows, sucker punches, improvised weapons, knives, grappling, taking a hit.
*Flaws*: a bad reputation, escalates everything, a bully, fights you should have walked from.

**Educated**
You had schooling most spacers never see.
*Edges*: well-read, languages, history, general knowledge, research, writing.
*Flaws*: student debt, arrogant, soft hands, out of place dockside.

**Famous**
You've been on the feeds.
*Edges*: being recognized, instant invitations, distraction, do-you-know-who-I-am, fans in odd places.
*Flaws*: recognized at the wrong time, sponsors own your image, an old scandal, needs the attention.

**Ghost**
Officially, you don't exist.
*Edges*: no records, a forgettable face, clean identities, living off the grid, cash only, slipping databases.
*Flaws*: no credit or standing, can't prove anything, papers never quite hold, someone did the erasing.

**Good Looks**
You have a face that opens doors.
*Edges*: first impressions, a disarming smile, camera friendly, working a room, being underestimated.
*Flaws*: memorable everywhere, jealous admirers, vanity, coasting on charm.

**Grew Up Shipside**
You were born and raised aboard.
*Edges*: free-fall instincts, ship sense, vacuum drills, sleeping through burns, tight-space living, every deck's shortcuts.
*Flaws*: uneasy under open sky, planet-blind, ship superstitions, a family scattered across the lanes.

**Heir**
You were born into money or a name.
*Edges*: the family name, trust fund, doors that open, expensive education, family retainers.
*Flaws*: strings attached, a kidnapping target, family disapproval, soft when it counts.

**Infamous**
You have a bad reputation, and you use it.
*Edges*: a name that opens the wrong doors, intimidation by reputation, friends in low places, distraction.
*Flaws*: a powerful enemy, blamed first, poor impulse control, the reputation is deserved.

**Light Fingers**
You have quick hands.
*Edges*: picking pockets, palming objects, the old switcheroo, card tricks, quick swaps.
*Flaws*: steals out of habit, greedy, a show-off, a record.

**Low-G Athlete**
You keep a trained body, tuned for space.
*Edges*: leaps and vaults, momentum tricks, endurance, coordination, throwing and catching.
*Flaws*: competitive to a fault, an old injury, sponsors own the name, a show-off.

**Old Hand**
You have decades in the black, and you're still working.
*Edges*: seen it before, a guy in every port, steady in a crisis, a long memory, knowing when to fold.
*Flaws*: slowing down, buried too many friends, set in your ways, old debts maturing.

**Out of Time**
You lost decades to cryo or a long burn.
*Edges*: legacy systems, history nobody remembers, debts owed from long ago, methods nobody expects, pre-corporate law.
*Flaws*: everyone you knew is gone, decades out of date, accounts long seized, cold rooms bring it back.

**Runabout**
You own a small craft: a shuttle, tug, or skiff.
*Edges*: fast, a hidden compartment, room for cargo, well-maintained, handles rough.
*Flaws*: liens against it, easily recognized, a temperamental drive, small.

**Small Frame**
You are compact, and you use it.
*Edges*: duct crawling, hiding in cargo, tight squeezes, being overlooked, light feet.
*Flaws*: outmatched in a straight fight, gear never fits, dismissed, an easy target.

**Syndicate Ties**
You are close to organized crime.
*Edges*: criminal contacts, a tough reputation, knowing where bodies are buried, moving stolen goods, protection.
*Flaws*: the syndicate collects, a powerful enemy, can't say no to the family, watched.

**Touched**
You saw something in the black.
*Edges*: sensing wrongness, uncanny dreams, steady when others break, knowing where not to go.
*Flaws*: episodes, nobody believes you, drawn back to it, sleep is no rest.

**Tough as Nails**
It takes a lot to bring you down.
*Edges*: shrugging it off, an iron stomach, pain tolerance, drinking anyone under, healing fast, rolling with the punches.
*Flaws*: ignores injuries too long, leads with the chin, punches first, mistakes toughness for a plan.

**Union Card**
You are a member in good standing.
*Edges*: hiring halls, strike support, brothers everywhere, grievance procedure, closed-shop access, the union grapevine.
*Flaws*: dues and duties, scabs are enemies, the local asks favors, meeting politics.

**Weasel**
You are slippery, quick, and shameless.
*Edges*: slipping away, talking fast, sensing a trap, playing dumb, technicalities, finding the back door.
*Flaws*: nobody trusts you twice, runs when it counts, a smart mouth, small-time thinking.

**Wired**
You carry neural modifications. Take specific mods as edges.
*Edges*: boosted reflexes, sensory feeds, a pain editor, a chipped skill (choose one), a clock in the head, recorded senses.
*Flaws*: overload seizures, jack addiction, licensed firmware, dreams that aren't yours.

**Workshop**
You control a useful place: a shop, lab, bolt-hole, or greenhouse.
*Edges*: the right tool, fabrication, stocked shelves, a quiet place to lie low, home-field advantage.
*Flaws*: rent due, everyone knows the address, hoarding, can't work anywhere else.
