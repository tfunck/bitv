* Intended to be an industrial powerhouse. Now all now only bare minimum investment by Omnitech and Mithril.
* The largest source of employment:
1) Ore mined from Klastos is refined by Mithril.
2) Omnitech manufacture and develop new, experimental weapons systems here
* Most work for Omnitech or Mithril. Make little more than enough to cover their oxygen tax.
* Outside of industry, space station is left to rot by Omnitech and Mithril
* What does work on the Dream is cobbled together by ad hoc associations of the inhabitants.
