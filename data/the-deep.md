Plotting its own course around Ekluo, The Deep sees very few visitors. The android production facilities must surely lie abandoned and dormant. And yet: a military blockade by the STPA has cut it off from humankind.

Some say The Deep is haunted by ghosts and gods. Others claim to have caroused with smugglers who ran the blockade and returned with strange artefacts far beyond any art or science known to humankind.
