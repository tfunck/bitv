In the wake of the cataclysm, Legon pulls the celestial bodies of Stella Tenebris ever closer to its maw. And Legon speaks: an as yet indecipherable signal that has drawn scientists and religious fanatics from throughout the galaxy.

Both believe the signal may explain the crews that disappear from their ships, leaving empty hulls charting blind courses through the system.
